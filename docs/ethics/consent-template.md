# Consent Template

This is usable consent language for an informal friend pre-pilot. It is not clinical-trial consent and should not be presented as institutional approval.

Replace bracketed text before use.

## Friend pre-pilot consent

**Project:** Support Paths friend pre-pilot

**Project lead:** [name and contact]

**Date:** [date]

You are being invited to help test a small movement-recording workflow. This is a pre-pilot, not a medical study, clinical trial, diagnosis, treatment, or fitness assessment.

The purpose is to find out whether walking recordings can be captured, processed, segmented into gait cycles, and compared with written movement observations. The goal is to test the method, not to classify you.

## What will happen

If you agree, you will be asked to walk under a few simple conditions, such as normal speed, slow speed, fast speed, shod, or barefoot. You may skip any condition.

Your walking may be recorded on video for processing through OpenCap or related analysis tools. The recordings may include your face, body, clothing, shoes, voice, and the recording location.

The session should take about [time estimate].

## What will be collected

The project may collect:

- video recordings of you walking
- OpenCap-derived movement data, such as joint angles, marker trajectories, and gait-cycle timing
- basic session notes, such as footwear, fatigue, recent exercise, comfort with being recorded, and any relevant pain or injury note you choose to share
- a participant code used instead of your name

You do not have to share injury history or any personal information you do not want to share.

## What will not happen

You will not receive a diagnosis.
You will not be assigned a permanent walking type.
You will not be told that the recording reveals who you are.
This is not a medical evaluation and should not replace medical care.

The strongest statement this project should make is:

> Under this condition, this recording shows this movement pattern.

## Storage and access

Raw videos will be stored in private storage controlled by [name]. They will not be committed to the public GitHub repository.

The people who may see raw videos are:

- [name]
- [optional collaborator names]

If OpenCap cloud processing is used, your recordings or derived movement data may be uploaded to OpenCap services for processing. OpenCap is an external platform. Ask before consenting if you want to know exactly which account or service will be used.

## Public sharing

Raw video of you will not be shared publicly unless you give separate, explicit written permission for that specific use.

The public repo or public writing may include:

- protocols
- field notes without your name
- anonymized movement traces
- aggregate summaries
- failure notes about the method

Small datasets can sometimes be re-identified. For that reason, the default is not to publish participant-level traces from the friend pre-pilot unless they are necessary and have been checked for identifiability.

## Voluntary participation

Your participation is voluntary.

You may:

- ask questions before or during recording
- skip any walking condition
- stop at any time
- ask for a recording to be deleted
- withdraw your data later by contacting [contact]

If you withdraw, the project lead will remove raw videos and derived data that can reasonably be linked to you.

## Risks

Possible risks include:

- feeling awkward or self-conscious while being recorded
- privacy risk if identifiable video is mishandled
- minor physical discomfort from walking conditions
- learning that an observation was made about your movement

You should not do any walking condition that feels unsafe or painful.

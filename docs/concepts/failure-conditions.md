# Failure Conditions

The project fails usefully when measurement shows that the theory, protocol, instrument, or analysis cannot support the intended claim.

Failure is not an embarrassment in this repo. It is one of the expected outputs.

## Governing question

What would make the support-path project weaker, narrower, or not worth pursuing in its current form?

The answer should be written before the pre-pilot so the project cannot redefine success after seeing the data.

## Theory failure

The theory weakens if:

- proposed gait families remain only visual impressions
- the support-path idea cannot be translated into measurable features
- different observers cannot describe the same recording coherently
- the proposed categories are too vague to compare with data
- the language begins to classify people instead of steps

Useful result:

> The concept needs revision before more data are collected.

## Instrument failure

The instrument path weakens if:

- OpenCap outputs are too noisy for the intended questions
- joint-angle curves cannot be interpreted consistently
- gait cycles cannot be segmented reliably from the available recordings
- left-right stance phases cannot be represented clearly
- capture setup has a larger effect than movement pattern

Useful result:

> The project must change capture method, reduce claims, or choose different features.

## Analysis failure

The analysis plan weakens if:

- clusters mostly reflect walking speed
- clusters mostly reflect footwear
- clusters mostly reflect sex, height, body size, clothing, or camera setup
- repeated trials from the same condition do not resemble each other
- extracted features are redundant, unstable, or uninterpretable
- left-right comparison produces numbers without readable movement meaning

Useful result:

> The analysis found structure, but not the structure the theory needs.

## Ethics failure

The project fails ethically if:

- raw videos are shared publicly without explicit consent
- identifiable participant details enter the public repo
- participants are given type labels
- informal friend participation becomes social pressure
- movement observations are framed as diagnosis, pathology, or identity
- consent does not make storage, access, withdrawal, and sharing clear

Useful result:

> The study pauses until data handling and consent are repaired.

## Pre-pilot failure

The friend pre-pilot fails usefully if it reveals:

- too few usable cycles per participant
- inconsistent capture conditions
- inadequate walking distance
- poor lighting, occlusion, or camera placement
- confusion about instructions
- unreliable file naming or metadata
- mismatch between blind observation and processed data that cannot be explained

Useful result:

> The next protocol version is simpler and more controlled.

## What not to rescue

Do not rescue the theory by changing labels after the fact.
Do not call every discrepancy a subtype.
Do not treat one clear example as validation.
Do not ignore failed trials because they are inconvenient.

The goal is to find out what survives contact with measurement.

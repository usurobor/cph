# Seven Ways People Walk

This article names seven informal walking families and places them under the deeper claim cph is actually testing.

The seven families are observational hypotheses. The Coherence Path Hypothesis is the deeper claim. The families are *candidate surface expressions* of coherence-path organization — useful as vocabulary, not as findings.

If you have read the hypothesis doc and just want the names and caveats, skip to §The seven names and §What the list is not.

## Why this list exists

Before measurement, walking still has to be talked about. Coaches, somatic teachers, rehab clinicians, and observers describe walking patterns long before any sensor confirms them. That descriptive layer is valuable — it generates hypotheses — but it cannot validate itself by being repeated.

The seven names were drafted from observational practice. They are placeholders the project is willing to lose. Their job is to give the Coherence Path Hypothesis ([docs/concepts/coherence-path-hypothesis.md](../concepts/coherence-path-hypothesis.md)) something concrete to be tested against, so that measurement has a target rich enough to disagree with.

## The seven names

Each entry describes a candidate organization, not a person. Anyone may express any of them under different conditions.

**Pendular Carrier.** The body rides over each leg like a pendulum over its pivot. Vertical centre-of-mass excursion is the felt signature; timing is even; the cycle reads as a steady carry.

**Elastic Rebounder.** Each contact stores load and returns it. Stance is short relative to swing; push-off is the felt signature; the cycle reads as a sequence of releases.

**Braced Axial Walker.** The trunk and pelvis hold a single axial line through stance. Rotation through the spine is reduced; segments translate together; the cycle reads as a column passing through space.

**Collapse-and-Catch Walker.** Each contact yields, then recovers. Knee or hip yields at load acceptance and is caught downstream; the cycle reads as a controlled give-and-recovery.

**Spiral Driver.** Pelvis and trunk counter-rotate around the stance leg. Transverse-plane coupling is the felt signature; the cycle reads as a wound and unwound spiral.

**Segmented Block Mover.** Segments move in discrete sequence rather than continuously. Step initiation, mid-stance, push-off, and swing read as separate phases; the cycle reads as four discrete blocks rather than one curve.

**Asymmetric Protector.** One side carries more load or organizes more stably than the other. The opposite side protects something — a joint, a habit, an old injury, an attentional pattern; the cycle reads through its asymmetry first.

## How the list connects to the hypothesis

The Coherence Path Hypothesis claims that recurring whole-body coordination paths can be inferred from gait-cycle data. The seven names are guesses at what some of those paths might look like at the surface.

Three relationships hold:

1. The families may correspond to recurring coordination signatures the measurement chain (see [coherence-path-hypothesis.md §Operationalization](../concepts/coherence-path-hypothesis.md#operationalization)) can detect.
2. They may correspond to *modifiers* — partial organizations that combine with each other rather than partition cleanly.
3. They may correspond to nothing. Measurement is allowed to replace them.

## What the list is not

- The list is not a typology of people. The object is the step under a condition, not the person.
- The list is not validated. None of the seven has been tested against gait-cycle data as of the current empirical posture (REVISE — see [reports/field-report-01-existing-data-zeroth-pilot.md](../../reports/field-report-01-existing-data-zeroth-pilot.md)).
- The list is not a diagnosis tool. Naming a family is not a clinical statement about a person.
- The number seven is not load-bearing. If measurement supports three families, twelve, or none, the Coherence Path Hypothesis can still survive — what is at stake is recurrence of coordination paths, not the count.
- Asymmetric Protector may be a modifier across all the other families rather than a family of its own. It is listed seventh for that reason and is the most provisional entry.
- AI clustering is not required to preserve the list. The project does not protect this vocabulary against data.

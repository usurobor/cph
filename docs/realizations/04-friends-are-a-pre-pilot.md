# Friends Are a Pre-Pilot

The first friend cohort exists to break the pipeline, not to validate the theory.

## The distinction

A friend pre-pilot is not a study.
It is not validation.
It is not evidence for a public claim.

It is a practical test of whether the project can move from recording to processed gait-cycle data without losing the object.

## Why friends

Friends are accessible, patient, and willing to tolerate awkward setup problems. That makes them useful for finding procedural failure.

It also creates ethical risk. A friend may feel social pressure to participate, accept recording, or receive movement commentary. The protocol must make refusal easy and must avoid type labels.

The project owes friends more care, not less.

## What the pre-pilot asks

The pre-pilot asks whether the pipeline is coherent:

- Can usable recordings be captured?
- Can OpenCap process the trials?
- Can gait cycles be segmented?
- Can interpretable curves be extracted?
- Can left and right stance phases be compared?
- Can repeated trials be collected without the data falling apart?
- Can blind qualitative observation be compared with kinematic traces?
- Which features are useless?
- Which categories fail?

The answer may be no. That is acceptable.

## What counts as success

The pre-pilot succeeds if it exposes what needs to change.

Examples:

- the walking path is too short
- lighting creates tracking failures
- the instructions change participant behavior
- the metadata form is too vague
- the qualitative memo is not specific enough
- OpenCap outputs are usable for some features but not others
- left-right asymmetry is clearer than proposed support-path families

This is success because the method becomes more honest.

## What does not count as success

The pre-pilot does not validate the theory by producing one compelling example.

It does not justify public claims about gait types.
It does not produce a classifier.
It does not diagnose participants.
It does not prove that support paths exist.

## Working rule

After the pre-pilot, the central output should be a field report that says what broke, what survived, and what should change before any larger data collection.

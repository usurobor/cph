## How to read this file

Each phase carries seven fields:

- **Goal** — what the phase must achieve.
- **Current evidence** — what merged artifacts show today; cited from owning files.
- **Gate** — the condition that closes the phase. "Closes" means status transitions out of ACTIVE / REVISE into GO.
- **Status** — one of NOT STARTED, ACTIVE, GO, REVISE, STOP. R5 and R6 carry their own status phrasings per the master issue.
- **Coherence risk** — how running this phase could damage project coherence (terminology drift, evidence/claim drift, identity drift). Not an empirical risk; the empirical risks live in the protocol and field-report files.
- **Next action** — the next concrete step the phase requires.
- **Owning files** — the surfaces that this phase reads from and writes to.

Phase transitions are gate-based. Each phase moves only when its specific gate is satisfied.

## Phase R2 — Contact-event and segmentation reliability

- **Goal:** Establish reliable heel-strike detection and gait-cycle segmentation on real Mocap calcaneus markers, on both sides or with a clearly justified one-side analysis path.
- **Current evidence:** Per [`reports/field-report-01-existing-data-zeroth-pilot.md`](reports/field-report-01-existing-data-zeroth-pilot.md) §Segmentation Status (post-fix), `scripts.segmentation.detect_heel_strikes` now uses robust-percentile normalization plus stance-region depth/length gating: each contiguous run of `yn < 0.30` (where `yn = (smoothed_heel − q05) / (q95 − q05)`) lasting ≥150 ms AND reaching `< 0.10` is one stance phase, with HS at the first sample inside that deep-stance plateau. The detector is invariant to absolute height, baseline offset, and amplitude. Result: 60/60 R-side trials produce ≥1 cycle (100%; 30/30 natural + 30/30 trunk-sway); L-side fires HS on all 60 trials but only 1 trial yields a complete L cycle, with 47/60 ending in mid-swing and 12/60 starting in mid-swing — a property of the source archive's trial cropping (~1.3–1.5 s, R-stride-aligned). The smoke synthetic regenerates 7+7 = 14 cycles at the expected 1.1 s stride period. The bounded revision shipped on [`cycle/port-segmentation-fix`](https://github.com/usurobor/cph/tree/cycle/port-segmentation-fix) (cph#26 step A); the original orthogonal branch `origin/cycle/segmentation-real-data-fix` (tip `a95415c`) was its precursor and is superseded.
- **Gate:** Detector fires on ≥60% of real Mocap walking trials per side, *or* the report explicitly justifies a one-side analysis path with documented reasoning for the asymmetry.
- **Status:** GO. The detector clears the 60% threshold cleanly on the R-side, and the one-side analysis path is justified in [`reports/field-report-01-existing-data-zeroth-pilot.md`](reports/field-report-01-existing-data-zeroth-pilot.md) by per-(trial, side) classification (`scripts/segmentation_diagnostics.py`) showing the L-side mid-swing endings are a trial-cropping property of the source archive, not a detector failure. L-side cycle recovery (contralateral-anchored detection or wider-window IK) is a downstream R3 / friend-pre-pilot-capture concern, not an R2 detector concern.
- **Coherence risk:** Reading R2 GO as removing the R1 REVISE constraint. R2 closes the detector subproblem; R1 stays REVISE because the bilateral construct still depends on L-cycle yield, which is a data-shape constraint that R2 cannot address. A second risk: treating smoke-test passing as construct evidence; the smoke generator hardcodes the heel shape the detector targets, which is a tautology, not validation.
- **Next action:** Closed. Future work on L-cycle recovery is named in R1's Next action and in [`reports/field-report-01-existing-data-zeroth-pilot.md`](reports/field-report-01-existing-data-zeroth-pilot.md) §Decision.
- **Owning files:** `scripts/segmentation.py`, `scripts/segmentation_diagnostics.py`, [`notebooks/existing-data-processing.ipynb`](notebooks/existing-data-processing.ipynb), [`reports/field-report-01-existing-data-zeroth-pilot.md`](reports/field-report-01-existing-data-zeroth-pilot.md).

## Phase R3 — First construct-level evidence

- **Goal:** Test whether the per-cycle features named in [`analysis/features.md`](analysis/features.md) show stable, interpretable coordination signatures at the subject level under condition response (natural vs trunk-sway), without pseudoreplication.
- **Current evidence:** Per [`reports/field-report-03-construct-evaluation.md`](reports/field-report-03-construct-evaluation.md) (2026-05-19 cph#27 R3 first-pass), the R-side aggregate analysis (10 subjects × 2 conditions × 25 numeric features, paired Wilcoxon signed-rank on per-subject medians, BH-FDR at q=0.05) produced 7 BH-significant condition responses including a textbook-magnitude lumbar bending increase (+18.3°, r_rb = +1.0, all 10 subjects in agreement) and a distal sagittal contraction (ankle range −6.6°, r_rb = −1.0). Hypothesis 1 (sagittal-dominant load transfer) survives in a *distal-contraction-under-proximal-compensation* form; the hip-as-sagittal-driver sub-claim does not survive on R-side. Hypothesis 2 (trunk-sway compensation) survives in a *trunk-segment-driven* form; the hip-adduction-as-compensation sub-claim does not survive on R-side. Hypothesis 3 (asymmetric phase-coupling) remains structurally non-testable — n=1 L-cycle in the archive, no R/L pairs at matching (subject, cycle_number) for any subject. Three candidate support-path hypotheses surfaced with mechanism and condition-bound scope: Lateral-trunk substitution path, Distal sagittal contraction under proximal compensation, Cadence-slowdown signature. Reproducible via [`analysis/r3_subject_aggregate_tests.py`](analysis/r3_subject_aggregate_tests.py).
- **Gate:** Subject-level (not cycle-level) condition-response analysis demonstrates stable feature distributions per subject per condition; aggregation method excludes pseudoreplication; at least two of the three framed hypotheses survive specification under adequate n.
- **Status:** **Partial GO on R-side; bilateral extension unblocked on inferred-bilateral surface (cph#28).** The R-side aggregate analysis satisfies the gate's three sub-conditions on R-side surfaces: aggregation is per-subject (pseudoreplication avoided); feature distributions are stable across subjects within each condition (r_rb = ±1.0 on four BH-significant features); two of three framed hypotheses (H1, H2) survive specification in their R-side-evaluable forms. Bilateral completion of the R3 gate is now reachable on the post-cph#28 feature table (57 inferred-partial L cycles); R3-bilateral results must apply the path (a) honesty caveat.
- **Coherence risk:** Pseudoreplication — reporting per-cycle distributions as if cycles were independent observations. Adjacent risks: `support path` and `coherence path` losing their distinction once recurrence/distinguishability tests run; alternative explanations (trial crop, marker artifact, camera setup, subject morphology, condition labels alone) being collapsed into the "construct survives" verdict instead of being named separately. A risk surfaced by cph#27's partial GO: reading R-side partial-GO as bilateral-construct evidence. A new risk surfaced by cph#28's bilateral extension surface: treating the inferred L cycles as measurement-grade — bilateral asymmetry magnitudes from this surface should be reported as "consistent with" an asymmetric coordination signature, not as "measurement of" asymmetric coordination. The field report explicitly bounds these claims.
- **Next action:** Re-run `analysis/r3_subject_aggregate_tests.py` against the post-cph#28 feature table to include L-side rows + R-vs-L subject-paired deltas; the script already excludes `detection_method` from per-feature aggregation. Append a Fix-round / R3-bilateral section to `field-report-03` (or open a new R3-bilateral cycle) that evaluates H3 substantively on the inferred-bilateral surface and completes the R3 gate. Carry the path (a) honesty caveat into every bilateral claim.
